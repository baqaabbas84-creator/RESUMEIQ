# 🚀 ResumeIQ

### AI-Powered Resume Analysis & Interview Preparation Platform

ResumeIQ is an AI-powered career platform designed to help job seekers **analyze their resumes, improve ATS compatibility, identify skill gaps, and prepare for technical interviews** — all from one place.

> **Build a better resume. Discover your gaps. Prepare smarter. Get interview-ready.**

---

## ✨ Why ResumeIQ?

Getting a job is not only about having a good resume.

Your resume needs to:

- 📄 Pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)
- 🎯 Match the target job role
- 🧠 Highlight relevant skills
- 📊 Have strong keywords
- 💬 Prepare you for interview questions

ResumeIQ brings these steps together into a single platform.
